# Skins V2 — pixel art plus sérieux, armes détaillées

Huit propositions générées avec l’outil intégré ImageGen, en prenant les images de skins-v1 comme références. Visages plus adultes, proportions moins chibi ; arcs et carquois, épées et boucliers, bâtons à cristal pour les mages, bâtons de combat pour les moines.

Propositions graphiques non intégrées au jeu. Adaptation au format de la carte et vérification à petite taille encore nécessaires.

## Prompts exacts

### archer_masculin

Use case: style-transfer. Edit the attached version-1 Alcazan Forest map character into version 2: archer masculin. Input is the identity and outfit reference. Keep the same recognizable gender, hairstyle, costume, main palette and class. User requests more serious pixel art and special care for weapons.
Style: mature classic tactical fantasy RPG sprite, restrained and grounded, adult face with smaller eyes, calm determined expression, less chibi and less cute. Head approximately one quarter of total body height. Clearly hand-placed square pixel clusters and hard pixel edges, restrained palette and shading, no smooth painted gradients, no 3D. Slight top-down frontal view appropriate for a tile-map character. Design readable at a logical 64x64 pixel resolution, show enlarged pixel art.
Weapon requirements: A clearly recognizable practical wooden recurve bow held vertically in the left hand, with two balanced curved limbs and ONE taut thin bowstring visibly connecting the two tips. Hand wraps correctly around the central grip. Bow is distinct from body, entire bow visible. Small quiver on back with clearly feathered arrows. No arrow being drawn, no floating bow, no extra weapon.
Composition: exactly ONE full-body standing character including feet and full weapon, centered with comfortable clear margins; equipment must fit in canvas. Keep hands, weapon grip and weapon silhouette clean and structurally convincing. Clothing practical, no exaggerated sexualization. Transparent background with NO glow, NO halo, NO vignette, NO cast shadow backdrop, NO platform, NO scenery, NO text. This is a revised game-sprite concept, not a portrait.
